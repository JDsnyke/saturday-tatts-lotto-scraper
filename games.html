Use the probability model that matches the game

Repeated draws, Keno spots and ordered digits are different experiments. These tools keep those distinctions explicit.

Set for Life

Single draw vs repeated draws

A standard Set for Life purchase covers seven consecutive daily draws. The per-draw Division 1 chance remains 1 in 38,320,568; cumulative probability across independent draws is 1-(1-p)^d.

Mechanics

One catalogue, several probability families

The Saturday portfolio optimiser stays specialised. The shared catalogue models each probability experiment before any strategy is discussed.

Combination

One-pool draw

Saturday Lotto, Weekday Windfall, Oz Lotto, Set for Life and Millionaire Medley use hypergeometric main/supplementary match distributions with game-specific prize mappings.

Two pools

Powerball

The seven-number main selection and the separate one-from-20 Powerball result multiply into a joint distribution.

Ordered

Digits and positions

Super 66 and Cash 3 use ordered digits; Lotto Strike uses ordered numbers sampled without replacement. Combination formulas would be wrong.

Variable

Raffles and instant games

Lucky Lotteries, charity raffles and scratch products need pool or print-run-specific metadata. Where a fixed experiment is not established, the lab reports rather than invents.

i
Interpretation

Easier top-prize odds do not automatically mean better value.

Ticket cost, prize amounts, payout rules, jackpot sharing, annuity terms and prize inventory all matter. The project does not treat lottery play as an investment.
