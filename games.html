Single draw vs repeated draws

A standard Set for Life purchase covers seven consecutive daily draws. The per-draw Division 1 chance remains 1 in 38,320,568; cumulative probability across independent draws is 1-(1-p)^d.

Architecture

One platform, several probability families

The Saturday optimiser remains specialised. The reusable layer underneath it now models the probability experiment before any strategy is discussed.

COMBINATION

One-pool draw

Saturday Lotto, Weekday Windfall, Oz Lotto, Set for Life and Millionaire Medley use hypergeometric main/supplementary match distributions with game-specific prize mappings.

TWO POOLS

Powerball

The seven-number main selection and the separate one-from-20 Powerball result multiply into a joint distribution.

ORDERED

Digits and positions

Super 66 and Cash 3 use ordered digits; Lotto Strike uses ordered numbers sampled without replacement. Combination formulas would be wrong.

VARIABLE

Raffles and instant games

Lucky Lotteries, charity raffles and scratch products need pool/print-run-specific metadata. Where a fixed experiment is not established, the lab reports rather than invents.

i
Responsible interpretation

A different game can have easier top-prize odds and still be a worse value proposition.

Odds must be considered with ticket cost, prize amounts, payout rules, jackpot sharing, annuity terms and available prize inventory. The project does not recommend increasing lottery spend and does not treat any lottery as an investment.
