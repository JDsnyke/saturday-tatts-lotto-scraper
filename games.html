Mechanics

Several probability families

The Saturday portfolio optimiser stays specialised; the shared catalogue models each probability experiment first.

One-pool draws

Saturday Lotto, Weekday Windfall, Oz Lotto, Set for Life and Millionaire Medley.

Two pools

Powerball combines a seven-number main selection with a separate Powerball pool.

Ordered games

Super 66, Cash 3 and Lotto Strike require order-sensitive probability models.

Variable products

Raffles and scratch products need pool or print-run-specific metadata.

Interpretation

Easier top-prize odds do not automatically mean better value. Ticket cost, prize amounts, payout rules, sharing and annuity terms also matter.
