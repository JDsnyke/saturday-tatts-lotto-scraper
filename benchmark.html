How to read the evidence

Exact combinatorics overrides a noisy simulation whenever both answer the same question.

Pair, triple and quadruple coverage are exact structural measures. The reference any-prize probability is now computed exactly. Bonferroni remains a rigorous lower bound. Monte Carlo remains useful for exploratory outcomes that have not been solved exactly.

Σ
Exact any-prize algorithm

Count the six-ball draws where every ticket stays below three matches, then take the complement.

Each portfolio ticket is represented by a base-3 match counter with surviving states 0, 1 or 2. As the 45 balls are processed, any state that would give any ticket a third main-number match is discarded. The surviving six-ball selection count is the exact no-prize complement; subtracting it from C(45,6) gives the exact probability that at least one ticket wins a prize.

✓
Exact Division-4 theorem

If every pair of games shares at most one number, Division 4-or-better is globally maximised.

For two six-number games sharing at most one number to both match at least four winning main numbers, the draw would need at least seven distinct winning numbers. Saturday Lotto draws only six winning main numbers. Their ≥4-main events therefore cannot occur together. The portfolio union equals the sum of its single-game probabilities, which is also the universal union upper bound.

Interpretation guardrail

Probability-of-superiority is not “chance this strategy wins the lottery”.

It is the empirical probability that a randomly chosen portfolio from one generator scores better than a randomly chosen portfolio from another generator on the named benchmark metric.

Method

Exact mathematics first, simulation second

The evidence hierarchy distinguishes exact portfolio unions, rigorous bounds, simulations and descriptive structural metrics.

01Exact

Any-prize portfolio union

Complement dynamic programming counts every six-main-number set in which no ticket reaches three matches, then subtracts from C(45,6).

02Exact

Pair intersections

Two-ticket ≥3-main and ≥4-main event intersections are counted exactly from the shared, ticket-only and outside ball groups.

03Certified

Bonferroni lower bound

S1 − S2 remains a rigorous lower bound and a useful cheap optimisation objective even though exact portfolio evaluation is now available for smaller game counts.

04Optimal

Division 4+ certificate

If pairwise ≥4-main intersections are zero, the exact union reaches the universal sum-of-marginals upper bound.

05Simulated

Shared draws

Monte Carlo remains for exploratory metrics that are not solved exactly; every compared portfolio sees the same simulated draw sample.

06Rejected

No simulated-training optimiser

A prototype trained directly on sampled draws overfit its training sample and did not generalise. It is not shipped as a recommended strategy.
