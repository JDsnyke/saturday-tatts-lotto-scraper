Method

What each result means

Exact mathematics, simulation, historical description and experimental ideas are kept separate.

Exact

Combination probability

C(45,6) defines the standard-game Division 1 sample space. Distinct combinations add linearly within one draw.

Exact

Subset coverage

Pair, triple and quadruple coverage is counted directly. Repeated subsets are measurable portfolio redundancy.

Simulated

Portfolio outcomes

Monte Carlo estimates unresolved lower-division portfolio behaviour and reports Wilson 95% intervals.

Descriptive

Historical diagnostics

Frequency, pairs, entropy and z-scores inspect past variation but do not weight future tickets.

Experimental

Anti-crowding

Human-choice research may inform conditional prize-sharing risk; the heuristic is not Australia-calibrated.

Guardrail

No predictive scoring

No hot, cold, due, pair-lift, recency or machine-learning score is presented as a next-draw probability edge.
