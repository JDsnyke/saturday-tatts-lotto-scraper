The central statistical rule

A number that has appeared often, rarely, recently, or not for many draws is not “due” and is not “hot” in a way that increases its next-draw probability. This project keeps frequency and pair analysis for auditing and exploration, not prediction.

Method

Maths before mythology

The v2 engine separates descriptive statistics from decision logic so historical noise cannot masquerade as predictive probability.

01

Validate every draw

Dates, ranges, duplicates, main/supplementary overlap and cross-file consistency are checked before analysis.

02

Measure deviation

Each number is compared with its expected count using a binomial z-score. Entropy and χ² are retained as diagnostics.

03

Do not rank “luck”

Frequency, recency and pair counts are not converted into next-draw weights. That previous approach has been removed.

04

Optimise ticket portfolios

For multiple entries, balanced coverage prioritises least-used numbers and least-repeated pairs to reduce redundancy.
